## Survey (2026-10-21, at `93f5a03`)

- **TS:** `drag-tracker.ts:144` returns on any `pointerType` other than mouse or pen (`D78-2: no touch drag in slice 5`); the threshold is 4 px, the pointer is captured only past it, the ending click is swallowed at the window's capture phase, `selectstart`/`dragstart` are prevented while a press is live. No timer, no `touchmove` or `contextmenu` listener anywhere. `pointer-tracker.ts:97-103` decays `×0.8` per sampled frame after 120 ms idle (the D68-4 note in the code); touch `pointerup` already calls `leave()`. `options.ts:278-289` `validateElementOptions` allows only `viscosity` and `recovery`. `stylesheet.ts` has no `touch-action`, `user-select` or `-webkit-touch-callout` rule.
- **Rust:** `swell_rect` (`elements.rs:74`) swells only `INTERACTION_HOVER`; DRAGGED (3) does not swell; `hold_dragged` softens a dragged element every substep (D78-5). No engine change is expected for W84.
- **The risk the spike answers:** Chromium decides at `touchstart` whether a touch sequence's `touchmove`s are cancelable, from the touch handlers registered then. A `touchmove` listener added only when the long-press fires may get `cancelable: false` and be ignored (an intervention warning); a listener always present on the observed elements works but makes a touch scroll starting on them wait for the main thread.
