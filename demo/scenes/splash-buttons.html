Splash Buttons

Click each button to fire an impulse() with a different splash config. Droplets emit from the element's perimeter, fly off with the impulse vector + jitter, and auto-despawn after their lifetime expires or they exit the viewport.

What you're seeing: W43 (FreeDrop entity) + W44 (impulse splash) + W45 (auto-cull) + W56 (canvas rendering). Droplets are stored in the same WASM buffer as soft-body entities and ticked by the Rust physics core — they're not separate DOM elements.

Tip: Open DevTools and check (window as any).liquid to inspect the live instance. Try liquid.spawnDroplet({x: 400, y: 100, vx: 80, vy: 0}).
