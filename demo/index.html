liquiddom: fluid engine (slice 4)

The soft-body demos were retired in 0.3 (their last state is tagged softbody-final).

  • Acceptance scene: the north-star scene (spec §6), in the default renderer (WebGPU on a hardware adapter, Canvas2D otherwise). Add &rm=1 for reduced motion, &clock=manual&test=1 for the test hook; without ?renderer the scene runs Canvas2D, as the tests expect.
  • Landing page: an ordinary one-screen page, liquid on its buttons and cards (slice 4). Use ?renderer=canvas2d or ?renderer=webgpu to pick one.
  • Multi-instance stress: 2–4 instances created in one task.
  • Splash: three drops with their own viscosity and recovery, a pointer-only pool, splash all and shake (slice 2).
  • Material playground: tune viscosity, cohesion and recovery live; water, honey and jelly presets (slice 2).
  • Long page: a page about 5000 px tall; the cards far down park until you scroll to them and come back at rest (slice 6).
  • Under the page: splashed liquid passes under a paragraph's text; ?page=opaque, wrapper, isolate or relative shows the fallbacks and container mode (slice 6).
